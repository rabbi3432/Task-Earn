About Task Earn

Task Earn is a digital platform designed to organize online task activities in one place. The platform provides a structured interface for users, task information, account features, and support.

Clear Information

We aim to present task descriptions, platform information, and service notices clearly so visitors can understand what the platform provides before using it.

User Support

Users can contact the platform support channel for account, task, and general service questions. Support information is kept separate from advertising content.

Platform Principles

Transparency

Important service information is presented clearly and without misleading claims.

Privacy

We describe how information is handled in our privacy notice.

Responsible advertising

Advertising, where available, is separate from task instructions and is never presented as a requirement to click an advertisement.
